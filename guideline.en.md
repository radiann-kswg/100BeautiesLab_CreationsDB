## Violations

**All of the following acts ARE PROHIBITED AS VIOLATIONS of these guidelines:**

- (1) **Secondary works that excessively infringe on R18G.**
  (e.g. excessive ryona / grotesque / gore expressions to the extent that they cause physiological fear, use in horror works, etc.
  　**Reason: the author will not be able to endure the various shocks involved.**)

- (2) **Use in applications that may be judged to be malicious, and expressions that seriously damage the impression of the work.**
  (e.g. impersonating the author, reusing the work with annotations that significantly damage its impression, unauthorised commercial use, or acts that defame the reputation of others through offensive expressions such as hate speech, etc.
  　**Reason: the author may be deeply offended, which could hinder future creative activities.**)

- (3) **Use in ways that seriously obstruct the author's activities.**
  (e.g. attacks on servers, unauthorised reprinting or resale of paid contents, and use that significantly involves copyright infringement or ethical violations that would cause the work as a whole to lose credibility, etc.
  　**Reason: from the author's perspective, such acts are essentially equivalent to a misdemeanour.**)

- (4) **Use of the work in violation of other creators' guidelines.**

- (5) **Depictions that differ significantly from the settings described on the site below and that may cause confusion regarding the official settings.**

* [NumberTales Official Site](http://www.numbertales-radiann.com/) (JAPANESE ONLY)
  (Minor deviations will be tolerated to a certain extent, **but please refrain from creating secondary works that clearly deviate from the official settings.**)

※If you encounter any of the above violations, please first inform "RadianN_kswg" ("ラジアン(柏木主税)", e-mail address: radiann.kswg6631＠gmail.com). We will then take appropriate action.
